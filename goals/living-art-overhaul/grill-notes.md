- **Shipping:** `origin/dev` only. Success is the GitHub profile README preview for `dev`. Do not change `main` in this goal.
- **Jury:** Scored bake-off, then I propose the 4–6 from those scores. You can veto or override. Taste is the tie-break when scores are close.
- **Rubric:** Four equal axes — striking, readable growth, distinct, GitHub-fit. No CI-cost axis. No extra scientific-accuracy axis beyond readable growth.
- **Gallery layout:** Full-width stack. One artwork per row at maximum size, then that piece’s `<details>`. Not a wrap, thumb strip, or even table.
