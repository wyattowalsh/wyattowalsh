### `generate()` pipeline (`scripts/art/lenia.py:1751-1980`)

Contract matches `scripts/art/AGENTS.md`: `generate(metrics, *, seed, maturity) -> str`. Extra kwargs: `timeline=True` (default), `loop_duration=60`, `reveal_fraction=0.93`. **CI/GIF path forces `timeline=False`.**

1. `resolve_render_metrics(metrics)` then `build_style_dialect("lenia", metrics)` (`1782-1783`).
2. Maturity: explicit or `compute_maturity`. **`growth_mat = 1.0` if timeline else `mat`** (`1787`). Published GIFs therefore take the low-maturity fade path.
3. World palette via `compute_world_state` + `_build_world_palette_extended` (`1790-1800`).
4. Seed hash + numpy RNG (`1803-1804`).
5. Repos: `order_repos_for_visual_plan` → `select_primary_repos(..., limit=CFG.max_repos)` then **`_augment_primary_repos` which ignores `limit`** (`1810-1877`, `_ = limit` at `1060`). Every named repo becomes a seed. `MAX_REPOS=10` is a density baseline, not a hard cap ([`scripts/art/shared/constants.py:29-30`](../../../scripts/art/shared/constants.py); `select_primary_repos` same “full set” policy at [`scripts/art/shared/visual.py:162-173`](../../../scripts/art/shared/visual.py)).
6. Language mix, timeline window, daily series (`1822-1860`).
7. **`field_gain` / `extent_gain` pulled from dialect knobs** (`1862-1863`).
8. `_derive_dynamics` (mu/sigma, kernel profile, sim_steps, satellite_count, seed_drift) (`1864-1871`).
9. Palette, kernel, `_build_seed_specs(..., extent_gain=extent_gain)` (`1878-1902`).
10. Zero field → `_seed_organisms` → copy `seed_field` (`1910-1912`).
11. `_simulate` FFT convolution + Gaussian growth, `dt=0.1`, steps capped `[30, 140]` (`1924-1936`).
12. **Blend that kills CA mass:** `field = max(seed_residue, clip(field * simulation_mix))` with `simulation_mix ≤ 0.34` (`1937-1953`).
13. `_render_svg`: glowing circles above `field_threshold` (default `0.15`, lowered by activity) (`1956-1980`).

Config ([`lenia.py:83-104`](../../../scripts/art/lenia.py)): `grid_resolution=50` (cell 16 px on 800 canvas), `kernel_radius=13`, `sim_steps_base=60`, `field_threshold=0.15`, `seed_radius=4`, `max_elements=25_000`.
