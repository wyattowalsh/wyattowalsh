## Finding

The living-art CI lane is already **dynamic on keys, hardcoded on six**.

1. **Dynamic keys.** `prepare-event-art-inputs` exports `json.dumps(list(LIVING_ART_STYLE_KEYS))` as `outputs.styles`. `generate-event-art` expands `matrix.style` from that JSON. YAML does **not** list `inkgarden` / `topo` / … . After R2 points `LIVING_ART_STYLE_KEYS` at `SHIPPED`, shrinking the tuple shrinks matrix children, shard artifacts, and (via S2 budgets) assembler inventory.
2. **Hardcoded six.** `strategy.max-parallel: 6`, assembler step names `Stage exact-six living-art artifact` / `Summarize exact-six living-art artifact`, comments that say “exact-six”, `artifacts._validate_canonical_inventory` docstring, AGENTS/docs “six matrix children”, and tests that assert `max-parallel == 6` plus those step names.
3. **Fail-closed exact inventory.** Assembler calls `stage_living_art_fleet` which requires every `LIVING_ART_BYTE_BUDGETS` GIF. A retired key left in budgets (or a leftover `living-*.gif` on an exact-directory stage) fails assembly; finalize never writes. S2 must drop retired budgets before S3 comments; S13 must remove leftover assets.
4. **dev-only publication.** `on.push.branches: [dev]` only. Prepare + finalize `if:` require `github.ref == 'refs/heads/dev'`. Finalize push refuses `TARGET_BRANCH != dev`. P0/C2 stay on `origin/dev`; `main` is not a trigger and not a push target.
